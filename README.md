## Current Work

### Resona OS
An experimental middleware and control architecture for separating model-generated proposals from authorization to act. Current work explores schema validation, policy checks, evidence provenance, scoped authority, and auditable state transitions. Demonstrations and specifications do not establish clinical validation or regulatory clearance.

### Research
QOTE and early oscillatory writing are preserved as a dated research archive. Their metaphors and proposed mathematical thresholds are historical hypotheses, not validated requirements of Resona OS. The current control work can be assessed independently of those interpretations.
